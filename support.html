Common questions

Video won't start, or a channel takes a while to load

Check your internet connection, wait a short moment, then choose another channel and return. Video files are streamed from the Internet Archive, so performance can vary with the source and network conditions.

How do I get back to the current live position?

Use Go Live to jump to the program position currently scheduled on that channel. Pause/Resume lets you continue from where you paused, as long as the current recording remains available.

How do I navigate on Apple TV?

Use the Siri Remote to move through the guide and select a channel. Select the currently tuned channel again, or choose Watch, to view it full screen. Press Back to return to the guide.

How do I watch on iPad or iPhone?

Tap a channel in the program guide, then open the full-screen player. Use the onscreen pause, resume, and Go Live controls. The interface can vary according to screen size and orientation.

Why does the program guide change?

The app loads channel and schedule information from Spelunkr, so channels and programming can be added, removed, or updated without installing a new app version.

How can I support the original Spelunkr project?

Visit spelunkr.tv for the original experience and any current donation or support options offered by its creator.
