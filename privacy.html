Privacy Policy

Spelunkr TV is an independent companion application for Apple TV, iPad, and compatible iPhones. It is designed to let you watch the channels provided by Spelunkr without creating an account.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves preferences such as your favorite channels and most recently watched channel using local device storage (Apple's UserDefaults). This information is used to make the app easier to use. The app does not send these preferences to the app developer or operate a personal account system.

2. Streaming and schedule requests

To function, the app makes internet requests directly to Spelunkr's schedule and channel-data services (including its Supabase-hosted public data), the original Spelunkr website for clock synchronization, and the Internet Archive to stream video files.

When your device connects to these services, those providers may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, request details, device or app networking information, and timestamps. Any processing or retention by those services is governed by their respective practices, which are outside this companion app's control. The app developer does not receive a copy of their individual viewing logs.

3. No accounts, ads, or in-app analytics

The reviewed app version does not require registration or sign-in, does not include an advertising network, and does not contain an app analytics or tracking SDK. There are no in-app purchases.

4. Support requests

If you choose to email support@routed.com, we receive the email address and any information you voluntarily include. That information is used to respond to your request and address app issues. Support correspondence may be retained as reasonably needed for follow-up and troubleshooting. You can request deletion of your support correspondence using the same address.

5. This website

This support website is a static site hosted through GitHub Pages. It does not use its own analytics, tracking scripts, contact forms, or advertising cookies. GitHub may process standard web request data as the hosting provider.

6. Third-party services

The application relies on independent services for program listings and video delivery. You can learn more about these providers at Spelunkr, Internet Archive, and Supabase. For GitHub Pages hosting, see GitHub's privacy statement.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy if the app, services, or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated with significant revisions. For privacy questions or requests relating to information sent to app support, email support@routed.com.

This policy concerns the independent Spelunkr TV companion app and its support website, not the original Spelunkr service or the Internet Archive.
